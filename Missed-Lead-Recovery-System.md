# Missed Lead Recovery System
**Elevat AI — Ready-to-use workflows**

Instant access. Copy-paste into your CRM, email tool, or AI agent.

## 1. Missed Call Recovery Sequence (SMS + Email)
**Trigger:** Call ends without booking or answer  
**Goal:** Recover the conversation within 5 minutes

**Message 1 (SMS – immediate):**  
“Hey {{first_name}}, just missed your call. Still available? Reply YES and I’ll call you back in the next 10 min or book here: [link]”

**Message 2 (Email – 15 min later if no reply):**  
Subject: Still want help with {{topic}}?  
Body: Short + clear next step + calendar link.

**Message 3 (SMS – next morning):**  
“Last chance – I have one slot left today at {{time}}. Want it?”

## 2. New Lead Follow-up (first 24 hours)
1. Instant SMS acknowledgment  
2. Value email with one useful tip  
3. Calendar booking link  
4. Soft break-up message at 48h if no response

## 3. Quote / Estimate Recovery
- Day 0: Thank you + “any questions?”  
- Day 2: Social proof + limited-time bonus  
- Day 5: “Should I close this out?”

## 4. No-Show Recovery
Immediate SMS + reschedule link + “life happens” tone.

## 5. Customer Reactivation (90+ days silent)
“We miss you” + exclusive offer + easy reply YES.

## 6. AI Prompt Pack (copy into any LLM)
“You are a recovery specialist for [business type]. Write a short, human SMS that recovers a missed call without sounding salesy.”

## 7. 30-Day Tracker
Simple spreadsheet columns: Lead source | Contacted | Reply | Booked | Revenue recovered

**Implementation note:** Start with only the Missed Call sequence. Measure recovery rate for 14 days before adding more.
